About Me

I am an incoming PhD student at Universitat Pompeu Fabra (UPF) in Barcelona, supervised by Prof. Ricardo Marques. My research interests lie in physically based rendering and path tracing — in particular light transport, Monte Carlo sampling, and path guiding. My background combines computer graphics, computer vision, and strong C++ engineering, from Vulkan engines to large-scale synthetic data pipelines.

At Snap Inc. (Specs / Spectacles), I built synthetic data generation systems using procedural generation, agentic workflows, MCPs, 3D Gaussian Splatting, and VLMs; improved sim-to-real quality (e.g. ControlNet and augmentations); trained and adapted vision models for 3D scene understanding with ablation studies; and ran GCP pipelines (Vertex AI, GCS) generating up to 5 TB of structured training data.

At ViewApp, I engineered C++ Unreal tools and world-building pipelines, automated builds with TeamCity, and helped ship a game to Steam, PlayStation 5, and Xbox. At Partium, I worked on Docker/Kubernetes microservices, CI/CD, Elasticsearch search, OCR, and computer vision models for spare-part recognition.

I hold a BSc and MSc in Media Computer Science from the University of Vienna (graphics and vision focus). German (native), English (C2), Spanish (A2).

  • Bachelor thesis: CNN hand detection and pose recognition with a procedural Unity training-data pipeline.
  • Master thesis / GAMEON 2025: procedural planet generation, LOD optimization, and real-time rendering performance.
  • VulkanParticles — open-source C++20/Vulkan game engine with CI and unit tests (GitHub).

My inbox is always open — whether you have a question, an idea, or just want to chat, I am always happy to hear from you. I especially love discussing computer graphics and vision, and above all anything related to path tracing.
